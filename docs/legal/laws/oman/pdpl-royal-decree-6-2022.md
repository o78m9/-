## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Oman PDPL (RD 6/2022) replaced limited provisions in the 2008 Electronic Transactions Law. It applies to processing of personal data in Oman, by Omani entities, and (with extraterritorial reach) by foreign entities targeting Omani data subjects. Defines personal data, sensitive data (health, biometric, genetic, criminal record, racial origin), and creates data subject rights (access, correction, deletion, restriction, withdraw consent). Consent is the primary lawful basis. Cross-border transfer requires MTCIT-determined adequacy or appropriate safeguards. Breach notification within a period specified by the Executive Regulations. The Executive Regulations (Decision 34/2024) issued in 2024 operationalise the law: consent requirements, ROPA, cross-border transfer mechanisms, DPO triggers, breach timing (72-hour expectation). Penalties include administrative fines up to OMR 500,000 and criminal sanctions for misuse of sensitive data.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Article 3 — Lawful basis

> Processing requires explicit consent of the data subject unless the law expressly permits otherwise (legal obligation, vital interest, public interest, contract necessity).

### Article 10 — Cross-border transfer

> Permitted only where the destination provides adequate protection per MTCIT determination, or under appropriate safeguards, or with explicit consent.

**Aooda impact:** Vercel/Neon/Anthropic/OpenAI = outside Oman; requires mechanism.

### Article 14 — Direct marketing

Consent + opt-out mandatory.

### Article 19 — Breach notification

To MTCIT within timeframe set by Executive Regulations (72 hours).

### Article 25 — Penalties

OMR 100,000–500,000 admin; up to 1 year imprisonment for sensitive data misuse.

## Penalties

OMR 100,000–500,000 administrative; criminal imprisonment up to 1 year for sensitive data offences.
