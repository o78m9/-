## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Decision 34/2024 issues the Executive Regulations to the PDPL. Defines consent specifics (specific, informed, freely given, demonstrable, withdrawable), DPIA triggers, ROPA contents, breach notification format (72-hour to MTCIT), cross-border transfer adequacy methodology, DPO appointment triggers, data subject request workflow (30-day response, free first request), and security control baselines.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Consent + lawful bases operationalisation

### ROPA

Required for controllers and processors.

### DPO triggers

Large-scale sensitive processing, systematic monitoring, public bodies.

### Cross-border TRA

Required before any export.

### Breach 72-hour notification
