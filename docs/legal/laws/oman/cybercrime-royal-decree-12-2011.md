## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Oman Cyber Crime Law (RD 12/2011) criminalises unauthorised access to information systems, data interception, system damage, identity fraud via electronic means, financial fraud, and content offences. Penalties include imprisonment and OMR fines, aggravated for state and financial systems.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Unauthorised access

Imprisonment 1 month–6 months + OMR 500–OMR 1,000; aggravated to 1–3 years + OMR 1,000–10,000 if data altered/disclosed.

### Disclosure of personal data

Up to 3 years + OMR 5,000.

## Penalties

OMR 500–OMR 50,000 + 1 month to 10 years.
