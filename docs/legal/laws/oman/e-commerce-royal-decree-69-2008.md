## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Oman Electronic Transactions Law (RD 69/2008) establishes the legal validity of electronic records, signatures, and contracts. Recognises basic and qualified e-signatures. Governs certification service providers and electronic commercial communications: opt-in + opt-out for marketing. Personal data protection provisions (now superseded by PDPL 6/2022) originally lived here.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Electronic signature validity

Aooda pilot agreement enforceable if signing method satisfies authenticity test.

### Commercial communications

Opt-in + opt-out mandatory.

## Penalties

Administrative fines; invalidation of non-compliant signatures.
