## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Jordan PDPL 24/2023 establishes the first horizontal data protection regime in Jordan. Applies to processing of personal data in Jordan and to controllers targeting Jordanian data subjects. Creates the Personal Data Protection Council under MoDEE, recognises sensitive personal data (health, biometric, genetic, ethnicity, religion, political opinion, criminal record, children's data), and grants data subject rights (access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdraw consent, portability for digital data). Consent is the primary lawful basis; alternatives include contract necessity, legal obligation, public interest, vital interest, legitimate interest. DPO required for: public bodies, large-scale processing, sensitive data processing, monitoring. Cross-border transfer requires Council-determined adequacy or appropriate safeguards (binding rules, contracts) or specific derogations. Breach notification to Council within 72 hours of awareness. Penalties include administrative fines and criminal sanctions.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

> Consent + GDPR-style alternatives.

### Article 18 — Right to object to direct marketing

Absolute right; opt-out free; honored immediately.

### Article 22 — Cross-border transfer

Adequacy (Council whitelist) + safeguards + derogations.

### Article 27 — Breach notification

72 hours to Council; affected data subjects when high risk.

### Article 33 — DPO triggers

Public bodies, large-scale processing, sensitive data, monitoring.

### Article 40 — Penalties

Administrative fines JOD 1,000–100,000+; doubling for repeat; criminal for sensitive data misuse.

## Penalties

JOD 1,000–JOD 100,000+ admin; imprisonment up to 1 year for sensitive data offences.
