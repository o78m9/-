## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Jordan Electronic Transactions Law 15/2015 establishes legal validity of electronic records, signatures, and contracts. Recognises basic and qualified e-signatures. Governs certification service providers and commercial communications. Pilot-agreement enforceability is supported.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Electronic signature

Aooda pilot agreement e-signed in Jordan is enforceable if signing method satisfies the authenticity test.

### Commercial communications

Opt-in + opt-out mandatory.

## Penalties

Administrative fines; invalidation of non-compliant signatures.
