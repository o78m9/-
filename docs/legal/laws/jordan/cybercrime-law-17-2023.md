## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Jordan Cybercrime Law 17/2023 replaced the 2015 statute. It criminalises unauthorised access, data interception, system damage, identity-related fraud, and content offences (including "fake news" and content harming national unity — provisions widely criticised). Penalties include imprisonment and JOD fines, with severe upper bounds for content offences. Relevant to Aooda primarily for unauthorised access and personal data disclosure tiers.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Unauthorised access

Imprisonment + JOD 2,500–25,000.

### Disclosure of personal data

Up to JOD 50,000 + 2 years.

### Content offences (caution)

Risk if Aooda content moderation fails — bot-generated content that breaches the law's content provisions may expose operators.

## Penalties

JOD 2,500–JOD 50,000+ + imprisonment 3 months to 5 years; aggravated tiers for state systems.
