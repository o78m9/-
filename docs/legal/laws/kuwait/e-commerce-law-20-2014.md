## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Kuwait Electronic Transactions Law 20/2014 establishes the legal validity of electronic records, signatures, and contracts. It governs licensed certification service providers, recognises electronic signatures (basic + qualified), and sets rules for electronic commercial communications (consent + opt-out for marketing). It applies broadly to civil and commercial transactions concluded electronically, with explicit carve-outs (family law, wills, real estate transfers).

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Electronic signature validity

Aooda's e-signed pilot agreement is enforceable if the signing method meets the law's authenticity test.

### Commercial communications — consent + opt-out

Prior consent required for marketing; opt-out must be honored.

## Penalties

Administrative fines; invalidation of non-compliant electronic signatures.
