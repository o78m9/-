# CITRA Data Privacy Protection Regulation (Resolution 26 of 2024)

**Country:** Kuwait
**Statute number:** CITRA Board Resolution No. 26 of 2024 (revising original 2021 DPPR)
**Date of issuance:** 2024 (revision of Resolution 42/2021)
**Date in force:** 2024
**Regulator/Authority:** Communication and Information Technology Regulatory Authority (CITRA) — https://citra.gov.kw
**Aooda relevance:** HIGH — Kuwait has no horizontal data protection statute yet; CITRA DPPR is the de facto privacy regulation for ICT service providers and processors.

## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Kuwait lacks a horizontal personal data protection law (a draft is pending in the National Assembly). In its place, CITRA's Data Privacy Protection Regulation governs ICT service providers (telecoms, ISPs, cloud, app providers, data processors). The 2024 revision (Resolution 26) updates the 2021 version (Resolution 42) and aligns more closely with GDPR concepts: lawful bases, data subject rights, breach notification, data classification (public, confidential, sensitive, top secret), cross-border transfer restrictions for sensitive/top secret data, and DPO designation for large-scale processors. CITRA enforces; fines and licence suspension are available remedies.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Data classification

> Four tiers — public, confidential, sensitive, top secret. Sensitive includes health, biometric, financial, religious. Cross-border movement of sensitive/top secret data is restricted unless approved by CITRA.

**Aooda impact:** Aooda must classify ingested customer data; cross-border for sensitive triggers CITRA review.

### Consent + lawful bases

Consent default; legitimate interest narrow.

### Breach notification

72 hours to CITRA + affected data subjects where risk is high.

### Cross-border transfer

Approval required for sensitive data export.

### Penalties

Administrative fines; licence suspension; CITRA-imposed remedial orders.
