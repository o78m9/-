## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Kuwait Cybercrime Law 63/2015 criminalises unauthorised access to information systems, illegal data interception, system damage, identity-related fraud, and misuse of personal data accessed via electronic means. Penalties include imprisonment and KWD fines; aggravated tiers for state and financial systems. Notable speech-related provisions (insult, slander, harm to national unity) coexist with the data offences.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Article 2 — Unauthorised access + privacy

> Imprisonment up to 6 months + fine up to KWD 5,000 for unauthorised access; aggravated to KWD 10,000 + 2 years if data is altered, copied, or disclosed.

### Article 3 — Use of telecoms to commit crime

Up to KWD 20,000 + 3 years.

### Article 7 — Disclosure of personal data

Up to KWD 20,000 + 3 years for disclosure of personal data acquired via information technology.

## Penalties

KWD 3,000–50,000 + imprisonment 6 months to 10 years.
