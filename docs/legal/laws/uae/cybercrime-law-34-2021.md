## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Federal Decree-Law 34/2021 replaced the 2012 Cybercrime Law. It criminalises a broad range of online conduct: unauthorised access to systems, data interception, identity misuse, online fraud, dissemination of personal information without consent, online defamation, and incitement. Penalties are severe with imprisonment and fines up to AED 3M, scaling higher for state/critical infrastructure offences.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Article 6 — Unauthorised disclosure of information

> Imprisonment + fine AED 250k–1.5M for unauthorised use/disclosure of personal data accessed via information technology.

**Aooda impact:** Mishandling merchant customer data = direct criminal exposure.

### Article 44 — Use of personal info to defame/blackmail

Penalty up to AED 500k.

## Penalties

AED 250k–3M; imprisonment 6 months to 10 years; deportation possible for non-citizens.
