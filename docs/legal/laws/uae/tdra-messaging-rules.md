**Country:** UAE
**Statute number:** TDRA Regulatory Policy — Unsolicited Electronic Communications + DNCR (various circulars; consolidated 2018-2023)

## Summary (≤ 200 words)

TDRA's regulatory framework governs unsolicited electronic communications (SMS, voice, email, instant messaging) sent to UAE numbers. Senders must register as bulk senders, obtain prior consent from recipients (opt-in), include sender identification and opt-out mechanism in every message, and respect the Do-Not-Call Register (DNCR) for UAE mobile numbers. Promotional SMS may only be sent between specified hours (typically 7am–9pm). Failure can result in suspension of sender ID, fines, and consumer complaints. Banks, telecoms, and licensed marketers have specific tiered obligations.

## Key rules relevant to Aooda

### Opt-in / consent

Prior consent required before any commercial communication. Pre-existing customer relationship may qualify under "soft opt-in" but only for similar products/services.

### Opt-out in every message

Every promotional SMS/WhatsApp must contain a clear opt-out instruction (e.g., "Reply STOP" or shortcode). Opt-outs must be honored within reasonable time (typically immediate, max 7 days).

### Sender ID registration

Bulk sender IDs must be registered with TDRA via telcos. Generic "marketing" senders are prohibited.

### DNCR check

Bulk marketers must check the Do-Not-Call Register before campaigns.

### Hours of sending

Promotional messages restricted to daytime hours.

## Penalties

Administrative fines (typically AED 5,000–50,000 per violation), sender ID suspension, telco-level blacklisting, consumer claims.
