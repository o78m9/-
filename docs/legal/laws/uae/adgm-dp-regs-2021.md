**Country:** UAE — ADGM (Abu Dhabi Global Market) free zone
**Statute number:** ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021
**Date of issuance:** 14 February 2021

## Summary (≤ 200 words)

ADGM DP Regulations 2021 replaced the 2015 regime and align with GDPR. Apply to all ADGM-licensed entities and to processing of ADGM data subjects. Data subject rights mirror GDPR. DPO required for public authority, large-scale systematic monitoring, large-scale sensitive data. DPIA for high-risk. Breach notification 72 hours to Commissioner; "without undue delay" to data subjects where high risk. Cross-border transfer to adequate jurisdictions (whitelist) or with appropriate safeguards.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Lawful bases (Section 6) + consent (Section 7)

GDPR-equivalent. Consent must be specific, informed, freely given, demonstrable, withdrawable.

### Right to object to direct marketing (Section 21)

Absolute.

### Cross-border transfer (Sections 39-43)

Adequacy + appropriate safeguards (SCCs, BCRs, codes of conduct, certification) + derogations.

### Breach notification (Section 44)

72 hours to Commissioner.

### Penalties

Up to USD 28M for serious contraventions (mirroring GDPR Art. 83 max). Suspension, censure, restitution orders.

## Penalties

Tier 1: up to USD 14M. Tier 2: up to USD 28M. Plus injunctions and individual liability for officers.
