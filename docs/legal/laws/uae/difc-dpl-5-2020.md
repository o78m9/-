# DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020

**Country:** UAE — DIFC (Dubai International Financial Centre) free zone
**Statute number:** DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020
**Date of issuance:** 21 May 2020
**Date in force:** 1 July 2020 (3-month grace through 1 October 2020)
**Regulator/Authority:** DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection — https://www.difc.ae/business/operating/data-protection
**Aooda relevance:** HIGH — if any Aooda merchant is DIFC-incorporated, this law replaces the Federal PDPL within the zone.

## Summary (≤ 200 words)

DIFC DPL 2020 is GDPR-equivalent and was designed to obtain (and has obtained) adequacy under the UK/EU regimes. It applies to DIFC entities, processing in DIFC, and to controllers/processors targeting DIFC data subjects. Strong data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, object, decisions based solely on automated processing). DPIA required for high-risk processing. DPO required for public bodies, large-scale sensitive processing, or large-scale monitoring. Breach notification to Commissioner "as soon as practicable" — typically 72 hours. Cross-border transfer permitted to adequate jurisdictions (whitelist published by Commissioner) or under appropriate safeguards (DIFC SCCs, BCRs, certification). Fines up to USD 100,000 (general) or USD 50,000 per article for specific contraventions, plus administrative penalties.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Article 9 — Lawful bases (mirrors GDPR Art. 6)

**Text (English summary):**

> Processing lawful only if: (a) consent; (b) contract necessity; (c) legal obligation; (d) vital interest; (e) public interest; (f) legitimate interest (with balancing test).

### Article 26 — Right to object to direct marketing

Absolute right; opt-out mechanism mandatory.

### Article 41 — Personal data breach notification

72 hours to Commissioner; data subject notification where high risk.

### Article 26-27 — Cross-border transfer

Adequacy + SCCs + BCRs + derogations.

### Article 62 — Penalties

USD 100,000 max per contravention; aggregate exposure can exceed.

## Penalties

Up to USD 100,000 per contravention; USD 50,000 administrative; injunctions; censure.
