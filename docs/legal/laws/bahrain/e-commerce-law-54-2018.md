## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Bahrain Law 54/2018 establishes the legal validity of electronic transactions, records, and signatures. Recognises both basic and qualified e-signatures. Governs certification service providers and electronic commercial communications: opt-in + opt-out for marketing. Pilot-agreement enforceability is supported.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Electronic signature

Aooda pilot agreement e-signed in Bahrain is enforceable if signing method meets authenticity test.

### Commercial communications

Opt-in + opt-out mandatory.

## Penalties
