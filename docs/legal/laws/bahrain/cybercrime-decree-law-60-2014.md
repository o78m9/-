## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Bahrain Cybercrime Decree-Law 60/2014 criminalises unauthorised access, data interception, system damage, identity fraud via electronic means, and content offences. Penalties include imprisonment and BHD fines. Aggravated tiers for state systems and financial infrastructure.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Unauthorised access

Imprisonment up to 6 months + fine up to BHD 5,000; higher if data altered/disclosed.

### Disclosure of personal data

Up to BHD 20,000 + 2 years.

## Penalties

BHD 1,000–50,000 + 3 months to 10 years.
