# قانون حماية البيانات الشخصية / Personal Data Protection Law

**Country:** Bahrain
**Statute number:** Law No. 30 of 2018
**Date of issuance:** 12 July 2018
**Date in force:** 1 August 2019
**Regulator/Authority:** Personal Data Protection Authority (PDPA) — https://pdpa.bh

## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Bahrain PDPL 30/2018 was the first comprehensive data protection law in the GCC, predating UAE's federal law and Saudi PDPL. It is closely modelled on the EU Directive 95/46/EC (pre-GDPR), with modern updates. Applies to controllers domiciled in Bahrain, controllers using equipment in Bahrain, and controllers processing data of Bahraini data subjects. Recognises sensitive data (health, race, ethnicity, religious belief, political opinion, criminal record). Lawful bases include consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interest, legitimate interest. Data subject rights include access, correction, deletion, objection, opt-out from direct marketing. Cross-border transfer permitted to whitelisted countries (PDPA publishes list) or under appropriate safeguards. DPO appointment required for certain processing. Breach notification not explicitly 72-hour but "without undue delay" framework. Penalties include criminal liability for sensitive data violations (1 year + BHD 20k) and admin fines.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Article 4 — Lawful processing

> Consent + GDPR-style alternatives.

### Article 17 — Right to object to direct marketing

Absolute; opt-out free; must be honored.

### Article 24-26 — Notification of processing to PDPA

> Controllers must notify the PDPA of processing activities (similar to the old EU registration model).

**Aooda impact:** Aooda merchants may need to notify PDPA depending on processing nature.

### Article 33-36 — Cross-border transfer

Whitelist + appropriate safeguards + data subject consent + contract necessity.

### Article 60+ — Penalties

Criminal: 1 year + BHD 20,000 for unlawful sensitive data processing. Admin fines.

## Penalties

BHD 1,000–20,000 + imprisonment up to 1 year for sensitive data offences; admin fines for other contraventions.
