## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Qatar Law 16/2010 establishes validity of electronic transactions, signatures, and records. Recognises both basic and qualified electronic signatures. Regulates certification service providers and electronic commercial communications: prior consent required for unsolicited communications, opt-out mandatory. Provides for legal recognition of electronic contracts and records on parity with paper.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Electronic signature

Aooda pilot agreement e-signed in Qatar is enforceable if signing method satisfies the authenticity test.

### Commercial communications

Opt-in + opt-out mandatory.

## Penalties

Administrative fines; invalidation of non-compliant signatures.
