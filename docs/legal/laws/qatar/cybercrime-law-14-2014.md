## Summary (≤ 200 words)

Qatar Cybercrime Law 14/2014 criminalises unauthorised system access, data interception, content offences (false news, harm to national interest, religious offences), online fraud, and misuse of electronic means. Penalties include imprisonment and QAR fines.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Article 4 — Unauthorised access

Imprisonment up to 1 year + fine up to QAR 100,000; higher if data is altered/disclosed.

### Article 8 — Disclosure of personal data

Up to QAR 500,000 + 3 years.

## Penalties

QAR 50,000–QAR 1,000,000 + 6 months to 5 years imprisonment.
