# QFC Data Protection Regulations 2021

**Country:** Qatar — Qatar Financial Centre (QFC)
**Statute number:** QFC Data Protection Regulations 2021
**Date of issuance:** 2021
**Date in force:** 2021
**Regulator/Authority:** QFC Data Protection Office — https://www.qfc.qa
**Aooda relevance:** HIGH — applies to QFC-licensed merchants (a distinct regime from Qatar federal PDPPL).

## Summary (≤ 200 words)

QFC DPR 2021 modernised the QFC's data protection regime, aligning closely with GDPR. Applies to QFC entities and processing in the QFC. Data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, object) mirror GDPR. Lawful bases mirror GDPR. DPO required for: public authority, large-scale systematic monitoring, large-scale sensitive data. Cross-border transfer to adequate jurisdictions or under appropriate safeguards. Breach notification 72 hours to QFC DP Office; data subject where high risk.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Lawful bases (Art. 13)

Consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interest, public interest, legitimate interest.

### Direct marketing (Art. 25)

Absolute right to object; opt-out mechanism mandatory.

### Cross-border transfer (Art. 33-37)

Adequacy + safeguards + derogations.

### Breach notification (Art. 40)

72 hours.

## Penalties

QAR/USD fines determined by QFC; censure; restriction orders.
