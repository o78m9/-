# نظام التجارة الإلكترونية / E-Commerce Law

**Country:** Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA)
**Statute number:** Royal Decree M/126 of 7 Jumada al-Thani 1440 H (2019)
**Date of issuance:** 14 February 2019 (Gregorian)
**Date in force:** 7 October 2019 (180 days after publication)
**Regulator/Authority:** Ministry of Commerce — https://mc.gov.sa
**Aooda relevance:** MEDIUM — applies to Aooda's merchant clients (e-commerce service providers), governs their B2C disclosure, return, and electronic communication obligations. Aooda must enable merchant compliance.

## Summary (≤ 200 words)

KSA's E-Commerce Law applies to e-commerce service providers offering goods/services electronically to consumers in KSA. It mandates transparent identity disclosure (legal name, CR number, contact), pre-contract disclosure of price/terms/return policy, contract confirmation, 7-day return right for unfulfilled or non-conforming goods, prohibition on misleading advertising, and rules on electronic commercial communications (consent + opt-out for marketing). It creates a register of e-commerce providers at the Ministry of Commerce and provides administrative penalties for breach.

## Articles relevant to Aooda

### Article 14 — Electronic advertising / consent + opt-out

**Text (English summary):**

> The service provider may not send electronic advertising or commercial communications to a consumer without prior consent, and must provide a clear, free, easy method to opt out of future communications in every message.

**Aooda impact:** Every Aooda-sent marketing message must include an opt-out link/keyword, and Aooda must honor opt-out across all channels (WhatsApp, SMS, email) within a reasonable window. Memory of opt-out must persist across campaigns.

**Compliance gap:** WhatsApp opt-out keyword set ("STOP", "ايقاف") not yet documented per merchant. Cross-channel opt-out propagation not yet built.

### Article 6 — Pre-contract disclosure

**Text (English summary):**

> Service provider must disclose, before contract: identity, contact, price (with taxes/shipping), payment methods, return and refund policy, complaint mechanism.

**Aooda impact:** Aooda-drafted product offer messages must surface merchant identity + return terms; Aooda cannot rebrand as itself.

### Article 21 — Penalties

**Text (English summary):**

> Administrative fines up to SAR 1 million per violation; doubled for repeat offenses within 3 years; suspension of activity; publication of decision.

## Penalties

Up to SAR 1M per violation; doubled on repeat; activity suspension.
