## Summary (≤ 200 words)

The 2022 Companies Law replaces the 2015 statute. It introduces flexible Simplified Joint Stock Company (SJSC), modernises LLC governance, permits single-shareholder LLCs and SJSCs, allows non-cash contributions including IP and services, and aligns with the 2030 Vision investment push. Foreign investors can fully own most company forms (subject to MISA licence). Director duties, related-party transactions, and minority shareholder protections are strengthened.
