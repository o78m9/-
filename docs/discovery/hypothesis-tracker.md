## Master hypothesis (as of 2026-05-31)

> Clinic owners running 200-2000 patient practices in Jordan feel measurable pain about dormant patients (>90 days no visit), have tried imperfect manual fixes, and would value an automated tool IF it doesn't require behavior change from them or their receptionist.

## Sub-hypotheses (also being tested)

1. **Pricing**: They'd prefer a free pilot + flat fee over pay-for-results (attribution feels suspicious).
2. **Channel**: WhatsApp > SMS > email as the right channel for patient outreach.
3. **AI fear**: They distrust "AI writes the messages"; prefer "approved templates with personalization."
4. **Competitor**: Their real alternative is "receptionist + Excel + WhatsApp Web", not Dentle / Yolo / Dentolize.
5. **Decision cycle**: A pilot with no money asked = signs in <1 week; with money asked = >2 weeks.
