```

- **Source**: [channel — discovery interview NNN / support ticket / NPS / sales call]

- **Context**: [one-line — what question prompted this, or what they were doing]
- **Themes**: [tag from: onboarding / pricing / feature-request / bug / praise / churn-signal / competitor / trust]
- **Permission to quote externally**: Y / N / ask
- **Use for**: [landing copy / pitch / case study / pricing page / sales objection handling]
```
