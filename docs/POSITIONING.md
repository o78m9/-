# Aooda Positioning Doctrine v2 (2026-05-31)

> Rewritten after market-researcher report + 5 founder concerns identified by `cs-ceo-advisor` panel. Replaces the implicit positioning baked into landing copy through 2026-05-30.

## What changed and why

### Trigger 1: Meta 2026-01-15 WhatsApp BSP policy

Meta banned general-purpose chatbots on WhatsApp Business API. Only task-oriented automation using pre-approved templates is allowed. **Our old pitch ("Claude writes a personalized Arabic message for every patient") is dead.** Any account caught generating free-form outbound text will be suspended.

### Trigger 2: Real competitor is not Dentle

Direct competitive analysis (see `[[project-aooda-competition]]`) ranked Dentle / Yolo / Dentolize as the visible threats. **But 95% of our target clinics use none of them.** They use a receptionist + Excel + WhatsApp Web. That is the actual incumbent.

### Trigger 3: "AI" framing scares the buyer

`abu-khalid` (Saudi clinic owner persona) has consistently flagged AI as risk, not value. "تجي رسالة غريبة من النظام = العيادة تنحرج." The word "AI" in the pitch raises trust friction with conservative buyers. It should be infrastructure, not headline.

## New positioning

### One-liner (Arabic, headline)

> **شغل موظفة استقبالك اليومي — أوتوماتيكي، عربي طبيعي، 10× أسرع، بدون خطأ.**

### One-liner (English, secondary)

> The work your receptionist already does for patient follow-up — automated, in natural Arabic, 10× faster, with zero forgotten patients.

### Three-line value prop

1. **Stops the silent loss.** Most clinics lose 30-40% of patients without realising — Aooda finds them, before they're gone for good.
2. **Sounds like your receptionist, not a robot.** Pre-approved Meta-compliant templates personalised per patient — never freeform, never embarrassing.
3. **Pays only when it works.** Free pilot for 60 days, then a flat fee starting from 50 JOD/month with a recovered-revenue bonus tier.

## Why each change

### Headline change

Old headline made AI the hero. AI is a buyer fear, not a benefit. The receptionist comparison is concrete, measurable, and immediately makes the buyer think "I want that for my clinic." It also defuses the "why am I paying?" objection by reframing as labour multiplication, not labour replacement.

### Differentiator change

"Approved-template precision" is technically what Meta forces us to do anyway — so we own it as a quality story instead of pretending it's a limitation. "Zero forgotten patients" is what the receptionist + Excel actually fails at — paper trails fall through.

### Competitor framing change

We were positioning against software the buyer hasn't heard of. The actual mental competitor is "the way we do it now." Naming Excel + manual WhatsApp out loud creates instant recognition: "yes, that's exactly what we do."

### Pricing pitch change

Pure pay-for-results creates billing disputes (see `docs/ATTRIBUTION.md` for the model). New structure:

- Free 60 days = removes risk to recruit pilots.
- 50 JOD/mo flat after = predictable for clinic, recurring for us.
- Optional bonus tier = upside without weaponising attribution. Clinic opts in if they like the model. Most won't, and that's fine — recurring is enough.

### Trust posture change

"PDPL-compliant" is invisible to a 38-year-old clinic owner. "Your data stays in Saudi Arabia, messages officially approved by Meta, every action logged" is concrete trust language. Same compliance, much louder signal.

### AI mention change

AI moves from headline to "how it works" page. Buyer wants to know what they get, not how it's built. AI in subtle small text under templates: "كل رسالة مخصصة بدقة باستخدام ذكاء صناعي ضمن قوالب موافق عليها من Meta رسمياً."

## Pitch script (founder, 3 minutes)

1. **Opener (15s)**: "كم مريض بعيادتك ما زار من 3 شهور؟ غالباً 300-500. كم منهم رجع من غير ما تتصلوا فيهم؟ صفر."
2. **Stakes (30s)**: "كل واحد فيهم يساوي 80-200 دينار لو رجع زيارة وحدة. هاي 24,000 - 100,000 دينار راح من العيادة كل سنة بدون أحد ينتبه."
3. **Why now (30s)**: "موظفتك تذكر منهم 30 بالشهر بأحسن الأحوال. مش لأنها مقصرة — لأن العدد كبير، والنظام يدوي."
4. **What we do (60s)**: "Aooda يحوّل قاعدة بيانات مرضاك لرسائل واتساب أوتوماتيكية، عربي طبيعي، بقوالب موافق عليها رسمياً من Meta — كل رسالة بتوقيت ذكي، باسم المريض، حسب آخر زيارة، حسب علاجه."
5. **Proof (30s)**: "بدون تكاليف ضخمة، بدون عقد. تجرّب 60 يوم مجاناً. لو ما رجع أحد، ما تدفع. لو رجعوا 20 مريض، أنت قررت تشترك بـ50 دينار بالشهر، أو 0 دينار + نسبة 15% بس على المرضى اللي رجعوا فعلاً."
6. **Call (15s)**: "كم مريض عندك دلوقتي؟ خلّيني أوريك على بياناتك الحقيقية شو نقدر نسترجع — مجاناً، 15 دقيقة."

## Forbidden words (do not put in marketing)

- "AI" / "ذكاء اصطناعي" — except in fine print
- "Bot" / "بوت" — never
- "Magic" / "سحر" — never
- "Revolutionary" / "ثوري" — never
- "GPT" / "Claude" — internal only, never customer-facing
- "Chat" — Meta sensitivity. Use "رسائل" or "تذكير"
- "Marketing" — buyers think spam. Use "متابعة المرضى"
