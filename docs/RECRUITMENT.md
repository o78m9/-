# Jordan Pilot Recruitment Plan (2026-05-31 → 2026-07-01)

> Driver: founder-concern C5 — 31 days to Q3, no proven recruitment track record. Original plan assumed 10 pilots in 14 days is easy. It is not. This doc replaces wishful with realistic.

## Goal (revised)

- **5 signed pilots by 2026-06-30.** Not 10. Higher quality, less scatter, easier to support per-clinic.
- 3 of the 5 producing measurable reactivation data by Q3 launch (2026-07-01).
- 1 of the 5 turning into a published case study by end of July.

Stretch goal: 10 by 2026-07-15.

## Why we lowered the target

Original target = 10 pilots in 14 days. Cold-outreach math for MENA B2B:

```
Cold WhatsApp outreach            5–10% reply rate
Reply → booked demo               30–50%
Demo → pilot signed               20–40%
Therefore to land 10 pilots:      100–200 outreach contacts
                                  ~30–60 demos to run
                                  ~2–4 weeks of recruitment alone
```

Add 4 weeks for onboarding + first campaign + measurement = **6 weeks realistic, 31 days available.** Lowering to 5 pilots leaves time to actually support them.

## Funnel math (target)

| Stage                       | Conversion      | N for 5 pilots | N for 10 (stretch) |
| --------------------------- | --------------- | -------------- | ------------------ |
| List built                  | —               | 60             | 120                |
| Initial outreach sent       | 100%            | 60             | 120                |
| Reply (positive or curious) | 25%\*           | 15             | 30                 |
| Demo booked                 | 50%             | 8              | 15                 |
| Demo shown                  | 80% (no-shows)  | 6              | 12                 |
| Pilot signed                | 80% (warm pool) | 5              | 10                 |

\* 25% reply rate is realistic ONLY for warm + Tier-1 outreach. Cold gets 5-10%. Plan A = warm-first. Cold pool is plan B only.

## Pool construction (priority order)

### Tier 0 — Personal warm (immediate, today)

Clinics where the founder knows the owner personally — by name, not LinkedIn.

- Existing customers from prior ventures
- Friends and family who own clinics
- Friends-of-friends with one degree of separation that founder can ask for warm intro
- Founder's own dentist / dermatologist / physio

**Target**: 8 names. **Expected conversion**: 60%+ to pilot (the warm intro premium).

### Tier 1 — Aooda demo-clinic alumni / inbound interest

Anyone who has touched the existing demo URL, signed up for the waitlist, or messaged the founder about Aooda over the past 12 months.

**Target**: pull from analytics + email + WhatsApp history. **Expected**: 15-25 names, 20% conversion.

### Tier 2 — JDA membership directory (semi-warm)

Jordanian Dental Association published member list. Filter by:

- Practice size > 1 doctor (excludes very small)
- Located in Amman / Irbid / Zarqa (high-density metros)
- Active web/social presence (sign of marketing-aware buyer)

**Target**: 30-40 names. **Expected**: 10-15% conversion (warmer than cold because of JDA shared context).

### Tier 3 — Cold (last resort)

Google Maps + Instagram clinic accounts. Use ONLY if Tiers 0-2 underdeliver.

**Target**: not built until Day 10. **Expected**: 5% conversion.

## Plan B — Recruitment underdelivers

**If by Day 14 we have <3 signed pilots, STOP recruitment for 48 hours and review:**

1. Is the pitch wrong? Re-watch the demo recordings — where does the buyer disengage?
2. Is the target wrong? Are dental clinics actually a hard sell for this? Should we test aesthetic / physio?
3. Is the pricing wrong? Are we losing on the 50 JOD/mo? Try free + Hybrid only.
4. Is the channel wrong? WhatsApp vs phone vs in-person — which gets a demo booked?

Decision matrix at Day 14:

| Signed pilots | Action                                                                                       |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 4+            | Continue plan, push for 5 by Day 21                                                          |
| 2-3           | Pause, run 5 founder-interviews with NO-deciders, adjust pitch, restart Day 17               |
| 0-1           | Major pivot — pitch is broken, vertical is broken, or buyer is wrong. Open question to user. |

**If by Day 21 we have <4 signed, DELAY Q3 launch.** Better to launch with 1 case study + 5 pilots in mid-July than launch with hype in July and burn out.

## What "signed" means

For the purposes of this plan, "signed" =

1. Signed pilot agreement (PDF in DocuSign / Adobe Sign / WhatsApp confirmation)
2. CSV of dormant patients delivered to Aooda (or scheduled date for delivery within 7 days)
3. WhatsApp Business number confirmed on BSP
4. Single point of contact named at clinic (decision-maker, not just receptionist)

Without all 4, it's a "pending" not a "signed."

## Outreach material requirements

See `docs/outreach/templates.md` for the actual scripts. Key constraints:

- All copy in Arabic, Jordanian dialect for Tier 0-2
- No "AI" mention in initial outreach (founder concern C3)
- One-line subject / opener that earns 5 seconds of attention
- ROI hook in first 2 lines ("كم مريض ضائع من قاعدة بياناتك؟")
- Demo offer in last 2 lines, no marketing softening
- Founder name + photo + clinic-owner-friendly bio link

Update daily.

## Conversion KPIs to watch (weekly)

- Outreach → reply: target ≥20% (warm) or ≥5% (cold)
- Reply → demo: target ≥40%
- Demo → pilot: target ≥60% (warm pool)
- Time-to-demo: target ≤3 days from first reply
- Time-to-signed: target ≤7 days from demo

## What success looks like by 2026-07-01

```
✓ 5 signed pilots (3 dental, 1 aesthetic, 1 physio diversification)
✓ 3 pilots have run their first campaign and have measurable visits
✓ 1 case study published in Arabic with permission to use clinic name
✓ Founder pitch validated by 5 yeses + 15-25 nos with clear "why no" data
✓ JDA contact established for Q3 expansion push
✓ Q3 public launch sequenced with case study + announcement
```

```

✗ No published case study
✗ Pitch still feels uncertain
✗ JDA not engaged
→ Decision: launch delayed, full week of customer-discovery interviews,
   pitch + pricing + ICP all re-tested
```
